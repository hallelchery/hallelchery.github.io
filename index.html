Sensing Glove

PS70 Final Project

A wearable glove classifying five hand positions in real time on an ESP32. Velostat pressure sensors were hand-fabricated from copper tape and conductive steel thread, calibrated by bend-angle regression, and sewn directly to the glove. A threshold-based classifier drives one of five LEDs per position: Hi, Point, Thumbs Up, Ok, Peace.
