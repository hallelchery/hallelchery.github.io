Metal Music Machine

In Progress

A self-tuning, three-stringed instrument designed to compose and perform Indian classical ragas. The ES50 prototype implements a full pitch detection subsystem — electret mic, TL082 op-amp (up to 101× gain), 700 Hz low-pass filter, and autocorrelation on a Teensy 4.0 — with a machined brass worm-screw tensioning mechanism. Next: C++ finite state machine, string-plucking servo, and raga composition algorithm.

Sensing Glove

PS70 Final Project

A wearable glove classifying five hand positions in real time on an ESP32. Velostat pressure sensors were hand-fabricated from copper tape and conductive steel thread, calibrated by bend-angle regression, and sewn directly to the glove. A threshold-based classifier drives one of five LEDs per position: Hi, Point, Thumbs Up, Ok, Peace.
